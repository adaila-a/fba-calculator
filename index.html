Common questions

How accurate are the fees?

Referral rates use Amazon's published category percentages and FBA fees use the standard-size rate table for the US marketplace. They're close enough to make a go/no-go decision, but Amazon adjusts fees regularly — always confirm the exact number in Seller Central before you price a product or place a factory order.

What's the difference between margin and markup?

Margin is profit divided by the sale price (what percentage of each sale you keep). Markup is profit divided by your cost. A 25% margin is only a 33% markup — sellers who confuse the two routinely underprice and lose money on every unit.

Why does a return cost me more than the refund?

A returned unit usually can't be resold as new. You eat the landed cost, the inbound freight, and often an unsellable-item processing fee. This calculator applies a 1.2× multiplier on landed cost per return to approximate that, so your margin doesn't look better than reality.

Should I include ads in the unit cost?

Yes, if you run ads — and almost every new listing does. A product that's profitable organically but breaks even with ads is not a product you can scale. Enter your actual average ad spend as a percentage of revenue.
